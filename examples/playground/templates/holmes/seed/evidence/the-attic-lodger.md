# What Mrs. Abney heard
_Not yet found: what the attic lodger says if someone climbs to the attic and asks her kindly._

Mrs. Ruth Abney is past seventy and hard of hearing in her left ear only. She sits by her fire with her right ear to the stairwell, and the street bell is the clock she lives by. She told Lestrade nothing because he shouted at her.

- At about twenty to ten on Monday the bell rang, and a gentleman went up to the office. She heard his voice raised, and Garrow's.
- At five to ten the bell rang again as the gentleman left. Garrow came out on the landing and called down the stair after him: "And the rest by Saturday week, Captain, or I go to your father!"
- After that the bell did not ring again all night, until the clerk came in at seven.
- Some while after ten, though the bell had not rung, she heard a creak from the back of the house, then slow steps in the office below, and a sound of tearing paper. She took it for Garrow working late, and went to bed.
