# The office in Cutter's Lane
_Found: the house and the scene, as Holmes saw them on Tuesday afternoon._

- **The house.** Jonas Pell's chandler's shop is on the ground floor; Pell owns the house and sleeps in a room behind the shop. Silas Garrow has rented the first floor as his office for twenty years. Mrs. Ruth Abney lodges in the attic above the office.
- **Two ways up.** The street door opens on a narrow stair to the office. A bell on a coiled spring above it rings loudly whenever the door opens or shuts. The second way is a back stair from Pell's back shop to a narrow door in the office's back wall. That door is locked from the shop side, and Pell keeps the only key on his watch-chain.
- **Street door keys.** Garrow had one, and Hale has one.
- **The room.** One window on the lane, a desk, the clerk's stool, a strongbox of the old pattern, a gas bracket. Garrow lit the office by gas alone. There is no candlestick or candle in the room.
- **The body.** Garrow lay between the desk and the back door, struck on the back of the head with the brass ruler from his own desk. The police surgeon puts the death between nine and midnight on Monday.
- **The gas.** When Hale came in at seven on Tuesday, the gas was turned off at the bracket and the room was dark and cold.
- **The strongbox.** Open, with Garrow's own key in the lock. Inside: a cash-bag with about forty pounds in notes and gold, untouched, and a ledger in green cloth that lists debtors by surname, one leaf to each letter.
- **The ledger.** The leaves for L, M, N, O and P are torn out; the stubs remain. The H leaf is whole. It reads: "P. Hale. £30 advanced against wages, March. Repaid at 2s. weekly. £18 owing."
- **The drops.** A few pale drops on the boards near the foot of the back door, soft and smelling of mutton fat. They are tallow, from a cheap dip candle, not wax. Holmes has some in an envelope.
- **The handkerchief.** Cotton, marked "P. H.", found beside the body.
