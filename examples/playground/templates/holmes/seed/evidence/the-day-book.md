# Garrow's day book
_Not yet found: the pocket book among Garrow's effects at the police station, which Hale can explain if someone visits him._

Garrow carried a small black day book in his breast pocket, in which he entered each loan and payment by date. It went to the Rotherhithe police station with his effects, and Lestrade has not opened it. Hale, if visited in his cell, mentions it at once: "He never trusted the ledger alone, sir."

Entries that bear on the case:

- **Lisle, Capt. E.** £60 lent in September. Monday: "£20 recd. 9.45 p.m. Insolent. Rest by Sat. week."
- **Pell, J.** £200 lent in May on the security of his shop and stock. "Due Saturday next. Will not extend." Pell's name would have stood on the P leaf of the ledger.
- **Hale, P.** £30 lent in March, repaid weekly, as the ledger's H leaf shows.

Hale adds that he left his handkerchief on his desk on Monday evening; he had a cold, and kept a spare in his drawer, which is still there.
