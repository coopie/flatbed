# What Captain Lisle admits
_Not yet found: what Lisle says when he is pressed on his club story, or shown the lodger's account or the day book._

Captain Edward Lisle is a young officer on half pay with gambling debts. Pressed, he drops the club story.

- He went to Garrow's office at about twenty to ten on Monday to pay twenty pounds. They quarrelled over the interest, and he left before ten with Garrow shouting after him down the stair.
- He has Garrow's receipt for twenty pounds in his pocket book, dated Monday and timed 9.45 p.m.
- He spent the rest of the night at a gaming room in Panton Street until three in the morning, losing. He lied because he had promised his father to give up play. The cut on his knuckles came from a broken glass there; the doorman will say so.
- The Halberd Club's porter will say Lisle left the club at nine.
