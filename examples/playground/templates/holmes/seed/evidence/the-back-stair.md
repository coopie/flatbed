# The back stair
_Not yet found: what the door at the top of Pell's back stair shows when it is opened from the shop side._

Pell has the only key, on his watch-chain. He is slow to produce it, and says the lock will not turn. It turns at once.

- The hinges are black with fresh oil, and the bolt is bright where it has lately been drawn.
- The dust on the stair treads is scuffed with recent footmarks, going up and coming down.
- On the third step from the top there are two more drops of tallow, like those by the body.
- Asked about the oil, Pell says he oiled the door "against the damp, a week or two back". He does not explain why a door rusted fast should need it.
- On a shelf in Pell's back shop is a box of penny tallow dips, his own cheapest stock, with one burned down to a stub.
