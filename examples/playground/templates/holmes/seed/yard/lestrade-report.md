# Inspector Lestrade's report
_Scotland Yard's file on the death of Silas Garrow, and the case against his clerk, Peter Hale._

**Metropolitan Police, Criminal Investigation Department.**
Death of Silas Garrow, moneylender, 14 Cutter's Lane, Rotherhithe. Report of Inspector G. Lestrade.

1. **Discovery.** Deceased found at 7 a.m. Tuesday by his clerk, Peter Hale, 24, struck on the head with a brass ruler. Strongbox open, opened with deceased's own key. Cash untouched.
2. **Motive.** Hale owed deceased £30. Five leaves are torn from the debtors' ledger, among them the leaf bearing Hale's own debt; the other four were taken to disguise the purpose.
3. **Means.** Hale holds a key to the street door. No other entry is possible: the door to the back stair has been rusted shut for many years, on the word of Mr. J. Pell, owner of the house.
4. **Presence.** A handkerchief marked "P. H." was found beside the body. Drops of candle-wax lay near it, from the candle by which Hale lit his way.
5. **Time.** Albert Coyle, lamplighter, saw a light in the office at 10.20 p.m. Monday. Hale left Union Street at 9.45 and reached his lodging at 10.30: ample time, at a brisk pace, to go by Cutter's Lane.
6. **Other inmates.** Mrs. R. Abney, attic lodger, is deaf and could give no account.
7. **Other parties.** Capt. E. Lisle, a debtor, quarrelled with deceased last week. He was at the Halberd Club all Monday evening. A cut on his knuckles is from a cab door. Not pursued; the evidence points to Hale.
8. **Conclusion.** Hale entered by the street door at about 10.20, struck down his employer, and tore out the record of his debt. Charged with wilful murder.
